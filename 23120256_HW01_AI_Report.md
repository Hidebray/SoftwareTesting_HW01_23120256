### 1.0 Overview / Introduction to the Job Market 2026+

The QA/QC job market in 2026 is increasingly moving from traditional manual testing toward **quality engineering, automation, API testing, CI/CD, and AI-assisted testing**. In Vietnam, current job-market data shows strong demand for skills such as Jira, SQL, Selenium, API testing, test planning, and CI/CD. At the same time, AI-augmented QA roles are emerging, where tools such as GPT/Claude are used for test-case generation, test-data creation, failure-log analysis, and automation support.

Based on the **10 job postings collected**, the most common skills are **manual testing, test-case design, automation, API testing, bug reporting, Agile/Scrum, SQL, and CI/CD**. AI appears in some roles either as a testing target or as a productivity tool, showing that AI is becoming an additional QA skill rather than completely replacing traditional testing. The **QA/QC role mindmap in Appendix D** should therefore be updated to reflect this shift. Three mistakes identified in the mindmap are: **(1) treating manual testing as separate from automation/AI instead of an integrated workflow, (2) missing AI-assisted testing and AI-system testing as emerging QA responsibilities, and (3) underrepresenting modern engineering skills such as API testing, CI/CD, and cloud-based testing.**

### 1.11 Synthesis – AI Impact on the QA/QC Job Market

Across the 10 job postings, the most frequently required skills are **manual testing, test case design, automation testing, API testing, bug reporting, Agile/Scrum, SQL, and CI/CD**. AI can potentially replace some repetitive tasks such as **regression-test execution, basic test-data generation, and repetitive test-script creation**. AI can assist with **test-case drafting, test-data generation, log/error analysis, defect reporting, and automation-code generation**. However, AI cannot fully replace tasks requiring **exploratory testing, understanding business requirements, stakeholder communication, risk assessment, and human judgment when deciding whether a product is truly ready for release**.

**Conclusion:** In the 2026 QA/QC job market, QA engineers should combine strong **core testing skills** with **automation, API testing, CI/CD, and AI-assisted testing tools**. Rather than competing with AI on repetitive work, QA professionals should focus on higher-value skills such as **test strategy, exploratory testing, domain knowledge, critical thinking, and validating AI-generated results**.
